常見問題 / FAQ

購買鑽石後沒有收到 / I purchased diamonds but did not receive them

請先確認購買已完成，然後重新開啟 App。如仍未收到，請電郵聯絡我們，並附上 Apple 收據的 Order ID、購買日期及產品名稱。請勿提供信用卡號碼或 Apple ID 密碼。

Please confirm the purchase completed and restart the app. If the diamonds still do not appear, email us with the Order ID from your Apple receipt, purchase date, and product name. Never send your card number or Apple ID password.

如何恢復購買？ / How do I restore purchases?

鑽石屬於消耗型項目，不會自動恢復；如付款成功但鑽石未入帳，請聯絡我們并提供收據資料，我們會核對交易紀錄。

Diamonds are consumable items and are not automatically restored. If payment succeeded but the balance was not credited, contact us with your receipt details so we can check the transaction.

如何刪除帳號及資料？ / How do I delete my account and data?

如 App 內提供「刪除帳號」，請直接使用該功能。你亦可以電郵聯絡我們，要求查閱、更正或刪除個人資料。

If the app provides “Delete Account,” please use it. You may also email us to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data.

回報錯誤 / Report a bug

請提供裝置型號、iOS 版本、App 版本、問題發生位置及相關截圖。請勿附上密碼或付款資料。

Please include your device model, iOS version, app version, where the issue occurred, and screenshots if possible. Do not include passwords or payment details.

遊戲進度 / Game progress

如你使用訪客帳號，刪除 App 可能令本機存檔消失。綁定 Apple 或 Google 帳號後，方可使用雲端存檔及跨裝置同步（如已啟用）。

If you use a guest account, deleting the app may remove local save data. Link an Apple or Google account to use cloud save and cross-device sync where available.
